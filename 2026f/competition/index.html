Session 1

👻 There are $2^9=512$ many $3\times 3$ matrices whose entries are only zeroes and ones. The trace of such a $3\times 3$ matrix is the sum of its upper-left to lower-right diagonal entries. Find the sum of the traces of all these $512$ matrices.

🎃 Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{2016} (-1)^{1+2+\cdots+k}k.$

🕷️ Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{100}\lfloor \sqrt{k}\rfloor$. Here, $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the largest integer $\le x$.

🧟 Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^{a_n}} = \frac{1}{2^{a_1}} + \frac{1}{2^{a_2}} + \frac{1}{2^{a_3}} + \cdots$, where $a_1\le a_2\le a_3\le \ldots$ are all the positive integers not divisible by 2 or 3. (This sequence begins as $1, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, \ldots$.)

Session 2

👻 The Fibonacci sequence is defined by $F_1 = F_2 = 1$ and $F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}$ for $n \ge 3$. Evaluate the sum $$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{F_n}}{2^n} = \frac{(-1)^1}{2} + \frac{(-1)^1}{4} + \frac{(-1)^2}{8} + \frac{(-1)^3}{16} + \cdots.$$

🎃 Give a simple closed form expression for the infinite sum. $$1 - \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right) + \frac{1}{5}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 - \frac{1}{7}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 + \frac{1}{9}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^4 - \frac{1}{11}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^5 + -\cdots$$

🕷️ The decimal representation of $1/17$ is $0.\overline{0588235294117647}$. Determine the decimal representation of $1/588235294117647$.
